What can I type?
Substrings
starts with 01 · ends with ab · contains 101 · no two consecutive a's · does not contain 11 · only “abc”
Counting
exactly / at least / at most 2 a's · length at least 3 · length exactly 4 · even number of 0s · odd length
Arithmetic
length divisible by 3 · number of a's divisible by 3 · binary numbers divisible by 5 · binary numbers that are even
Positions
third symbol from the end is 1 · second symbol is 0 · starts and ends with the same symbol · starts and ends with a
Combine
use and, or, but, not, neither … nor — e.g. “starts with 0 but does not end with 1”
Alphabet
add “over {a,b}” to the question, or fill the Σ box. Otherwise it is guessed from the symbols used.

Anything unusual? Use the Manual builder tab — fill the transition table yourself and still get the diagram and animation.
