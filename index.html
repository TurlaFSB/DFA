Writing questions

  • Combine with and · or · but · not · neither…nor
  • Set the alphabet with over {a,b}
  • Regular expressions work: (a+b)*abb   (+ or | = union)
  • Set notation works: { w ∈ {a,b}* | |w|_a is even } or a^n b^m
  • Not regular (e.g. equal 0s and 1s)? I'll say no DFA exists and why.
